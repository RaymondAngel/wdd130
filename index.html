Raymond Amande | WDD 130

I am a creative coder and BYU student who enjoys learning and solving problems through technology. I have been happily married for 11 years to my middle school sweetheart, Saira, and I am a proud father of five smart children. As a quadriplegic, I approach every challenge with patience, faith, and determination, completing my coding projects using voice activation. My goal is to master Python fundamentals, maintain a clear and consistent coding style, and use my skills to serve others and inspire perseverance through faith and family. In addition to coursework, I contribute to small web projects and focus on building accessible, responsive interfaces. I spend time studying algorithms, improving accessibility practices, and documenting my work so it can help others. Lately I'm experimenting with layout techniques, responsive images, and progressive enhancement to make sites work well on all devices. I regularly test my pages with keyboard-only navigation and screen readers to ensure inclusivity. I'm always open to collaboration and feedback—feel free to reach out if you'd like to work together.
