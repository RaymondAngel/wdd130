About Us

About White Water Rafting California

Our purpose is to provide safe, thrilling river adventures while protecting the rivers we love. We train every guide to the highest safety standards, focus on excellent customer service, and partner with local communities to support conservation. Our mission is to make white water accessible to newcomers and rewarding for experienced paddlers through well-planned trips and expert instruction. We operate with integrity, respect for nature, and a commitment to creating lasting memories for our guests.

History

White Water Rafting California began as a small guide service founded by river lovers who wanted to share the thrill of California rapids in a safe, friendly way. Over the years, the company has grown into a respected adventure outfitter known for experienced guides, community partnerships, and an ongoing commitment to river stewardship.

Adventure Awaits You!

Every season brings new opportunities to explore California's rivers, from calm scenic floats to unforgettable white water experiences. Our expert guides make it easy to book the adventure that fits your group and skill level.
